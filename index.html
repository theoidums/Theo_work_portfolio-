Where art meets engineering

I’m a multidisciplinary creative with a Mechanical Engineering degree and a deep passion for visual storytelling. That mix lets me think like an engineer and create like an artist — whether I’m sculpting a character, boarding a sequence, or dimensioning a part for production.
